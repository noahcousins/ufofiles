## Overview

This archive of declassified government UFO/UAP documents ("we", "us") explains
here what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

## What we collect

- **Account data**: your email address and authentication details when you
  sign in (email/password, magic link, or Google).
- **Usage data**: analytics about how the archive is used (via
  PostHog), only with your consent.
- **Content you create**: bookmarks and clips tied to your account.
- **Technical data**: IP address and request metadata used for rate limiting
  and abuse prevention.

## Cookies & analytics

We use essential cookies to run the site and, with your consent, analytics
cookies (PostHog) to understand usage. You can change this anytime via "Cookie
preferences" in the footer.

## Marketing emails

If you create an account, we may send you occasional product emails. Every
message includes a one-click unsubscribe, and you can opt out anytime from your
[account settings](/account). We never sell your email address.

## Service providers

We share data only with providers that help us operate: Resend (email), PostHog
(analytics), Cloudflare (file delivery), and Google (optional sign-in). Each
processes data on our behalf.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete
your data, and to withdraw consent. Contact us to exercise them.

## Data retention

We keep your account data for as long as your account is active. If you request to delete
your account, we remove your personal data within a reasonable period, except
where we're required to keep it for legal or security reasons (for example,
abuse-prevention logs). Analytics data may be retained in aggregated or
anonymized form.
