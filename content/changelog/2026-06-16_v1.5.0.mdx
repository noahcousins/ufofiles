Pull the best moments out of any video into your own clips. Find interesting data faster with the Watch page.

<ChangeItem type="feature">
  Create clips from any video: drag the handles to trim the exact moment, preview it on loop, then save it to download or watch
</ChangeItem>

<ChangeItem type="feature">
  Find interesting data faster with the Watch page: scroll through a feed of videos, and seek the timeline for moments of interest
</ChangeItem>

<ChangeItem type="feature">
  Sign up for an account to save your clips and export your favorite data
</ChangeItem>

<ChangeItem type="improvement">
  Jump between files without leaving the viewer using the arrow keys or the next and previous controls
</ChangeItem>

<ChangeItem type="improvement">
  Save your bookmarks and clips to a free account so they're waiting for you next time
</ChangeItem>
